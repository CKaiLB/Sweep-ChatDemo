How to Use the Dashboard

  1. Enter the email subject in the Subject field.
  2. Write your message in HTML format in the Message field.
  3. Click Send Email to deliver your message to all recipients via the n8n backend.
  4. Wait for a confirmation or error message below the form.

Tips for Best Results

  1. Use valid HTML for rich formatting (e.g., <b>, <i>, <ul>, <a> tags).
  2. Keep your subject clear and concise.
  3. Test your message with a small group before mass sending.
  4. Contact support if you encounter issues.
